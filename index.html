Ergonet Demo Walkthrough

Ergonet is an autonomous multi-agent software development system in development for research and training purposes (not publicly available). It combines AI coding agents with sandboxed container execution (Docker), spend limits, and human review gates.

What Was Real vs. What Was Pre-Compiled:
The React UI, Docker container command execution, API spend controls, and human sign-off gates are 100% real.

The three software deliverables in the Outbox (webpage, slide deck, and game) were pre-compiled static files, not generated live by the models during this 3-minute clip. When agents are pressured to produce complex software under tight deadlines without real compilers, they often fake the output. We are actively working on fixing this with deterministic compilers.
